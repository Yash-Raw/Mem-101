# Context Assembly

Turning ranked memories into the text the model actually sees: choosing how many fit, in what order, under what framing.

## Why it matters in a memory layer

Assembly is where retrieval quality becomes behaviour, and small choices have large effects. Memories presented as bare assertions get defended against the user; memories truncated mid-sentence become hazards. Dropping a whole memory is always better than keeping half of one.
