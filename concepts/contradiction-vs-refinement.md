# Contradiction vs Refinement

Two ways a later claim can change an earlier one. A **contradiction** makes it false — *does not drink coffee* against *drinks three a day*. A **refinement** narrows it while leaving part standing — *vegetarian* becoming *pescatarian*, where the constraint on meat survives.

## Why it matters in a memory layer

Treating every change as a contradiction destroys constraints that still hold; treating every change as a refinement leaves stale beliefs live. The distinction cannot be recovered from similarity — the refinement in this corpus scores *higher* than the contradiction — so something has to name the relationship before anything acts on it. Same slot, same subject, opposite correct response.
