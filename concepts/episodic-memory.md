# Episodic Memory

A record that **something happened at a time**: Priya changed jobs in December, the deploy failed on Tuesday. Its truth is anchored to a moment, so it never becomes stale — it becomes *past*.

## Why it matters in a memory layer

Episodes are the raw material everything else is derived from, and the only memory type that is safe to keep forever: a dated event does not stop being true. Their weakness is retrieval. "I'm leaving Northwind, starting at Calico" is an episode; "where do I work" asks for a state, and the two share almost no surface form. Systems that store only episodes recall them poorly, which is why promotion to [semantic memory](semantic-memory.md) exists.
