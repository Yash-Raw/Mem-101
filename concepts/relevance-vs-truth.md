# Relevance vs Truth

Two different reasons a memory stops mattering. A belief becomes **false** when something supersedes it. A memory becomes **irrelevant** when nothing has needed it for a long time — while remaining perfectly true.

## Why it matters in a memory layer

Confusing them produces both classic failures. Treat fading as falsification and the system retires facts nobody contradicted; treat falsification as fading and stale beliefs linger because they are recent. They also decay at different rates by type: an episode is over the moment it happens and recedes steadily, while a standing belief is current until superseded and should barely fade at all. Supersession handles the first; [decay](decay-function.md) handles the second.
